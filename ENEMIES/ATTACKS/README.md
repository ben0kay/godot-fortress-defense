# ENEMIES/ATTACKS — ST1 contact damage

**Status: Source Implemented / Awaiting Owner Validation.** `EnemyMeleeAttack.cs` owns a cooldown independent of path search. An arrived enemy attacks only a live building within center-to-footprint range and with a clear contact line; it calls the existing shared `Building.ApplyDamage` scalar receiver. Attack intervals stay at physics cadence even offscreen. No catch-up hit bursts after long frames.

Damage/range/cooldown are authored in EnemyDefinition and snapshotted at spawn. Defaults: 8 damage, 24-pixel range, 1.2 seconds. Larger configured clearance requires a correspondingly larger range. Shields and generic resistance pipelines remain future work. EN2 adds the optional ranged executor below.

EnemyRangedAttack snapshots EnemyRangedConfig, checks full-footprint range, positive-radius clear sight and aim tolerance, then launches shared CombatProjectile hostile rounds from the visible forward barrel. Enemy retains movement/target ownership. Cooldown has no catch-up burst; shots resolve once through existing building ApplyDamage/ICombatTarget. No player hits, homing or pooling.

AIR1 adds EnemyImpactAttack: a movement-independent swept intended-footprint contact, once-only resolution flag set before damage, existing ICombatTarget damage and guaranteed clean despawn. Flight may cross unrelated structures without hitting them. The contact receiver can be reused after a future ground approach; no kamikaze variant or splash damage is added. Enemy003 snapshots AttackBehavior=Impact and damage15; other enemies default Contact with optional existing ranged execution. Flying contact/ranged hooks remain separate from movement; no orbit/transport behaviors. EnemyRangedConfig now explicitly selects GroundObstructed (unchanged Enemy002) or IntendedGroundTarget overflight for future shooters.
